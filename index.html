⚠ Critical Standard

Candidates must complete ALL events within 26 minutes and 30 seconds. This is a cumulative time — no individual event is timed separately. Any candidate who fails to complete within the time limit is eliminated from EIB testing. No candidate who fails the EPFA will progress to Phase Two.

Uniform Requirements

  • Ballistic body armor carrier with front and back plates (unit/CIF issued, min 20 lbs, nipple-to-navel coverage)
  • Army-issued ballistic helmet (ACH)
  • OCP top and bottoms, belt, coyote tan t-shirt
  • AR 670-1 approved boots
Events in Order

EPFA Event Sequence

1

1-Mile Run (First)

Candidates line up and on command, begin running. Must complete the designated 1-mile distance before moving to the next event. Walking is authorized but strongly discouraged. No physical assistance allowed — candidate will be terminated if helped or if they leave the course dishonestly.

2

30 Dead-Stop Pushups

From a straight-body pushup position, candidates lower their chest to the ground and lift BOTH hands off the surface (dead-stop). Push back up with arms generally straight. Knees must not contact the ground during execution. All 30 repetitions must be completed before moving on. Candidates may rest in any position but must complete all reps.

3

100-Meter Sprint

Sprint 100 meters to the sandbag platform area. This transitions directly into the sandbag lift event.

4

16x Sandbag Lifts

Lift 16 sandbags onto a 65-inch platform. Each sandbag must be lifted and placed onto the platform to count. Candidates must complete all 16 repetitions.

5

50-Meter Farmers Carry

Carry two 5-gallon Army Water cans (approximately 40 lbs each) for 50 meters. Both cans must be carried simultaneously for the full distance.

6

50-Meter Movement Drill

High crawl for 25 meters, then a 25-meter 3-5 second rush. This simulates combat movement under fire.

7

1-Mile Run (Second)

Same standards as the first 1-mile run. Candidates must complete the full mile to finish the EPFA. Total cumulative time for all events must be 26:30 or less.

  • Orienting the map using a lensatic compass (declination adjustment)
  • Determining azimuth and back-azimuth

  • Terrain association — using terrain features to confirm location
  • Night navigation — using a red lens and adjusted techniques
  • Using the protractor correctly to measure distances

M4 Carbine / M16 Rifle — Immediate Action & CLFC

▾
Task
Clear, load, fire until stoppage, perform immediate action, expend remaining ammo, unload, and clear an M4/M16
Standard
Correctly perform all steps, in sequence, in 30 seconds or less
Starting Position
Weapon in low ready. Weapon free of ammo, bolt forward, on FIRE, trigger pulled.
  1. Clear the weapon: Point safe direction. Lock bolt to rear. Pull charging handle rearward, hold bolt catch. Allow bolt forward until it engages bolt catch. Release. Return charging handle forward. Ensure receiver/chamber free of ammo. Place on SAFE.
  2. Load the weapon: Insert magazine (push up until catch engages, tap upward to seat). Chamber a round: press upper portion of bolt catch to release bolt forward. Tap forward assist. Place on SEMI and engage target.
  3. Perform Immediate Action (on stoppage): Remove trigger finger. Forcefully tap magazine upward with heel of non-firing hand. Grasp charging handle, retract fully to rear, release. Reacquire target. Press trigger — if fires, continue; if not, clear.
  4. Clear (final): Point muzzle safe direction. Place on SAFE. Remove magazine. Lock bolt open via charging handle. Allow bolt forward until it engages bolt catch. Return charging handle forward. Place selector on SAFE. Ensure no ammo in receiver/chamber. Return bolt to closed position. Close ejection port cover.
✓ GO: All steps correct, in sequence, ≤30 sec
✗ NO-GO: Out of sequence, exceeds time, ammo not cleared

M320 Grenade Launcher — Load, Fire & Unload

▾
Task
Load, fire and unload an M320. Unload and clear an M320 that has not been fired.
Standard (Load/Fire)
Correctly clear, load, and fire in 20 seconds
Standard (Unload)
Correctly unload and clear in 20 seconds. Must NOT drop 40mm ammo.

Load and Fire:

  1. Ensure weapon is in "S" (safe) position.
  2. Press barrel release and pivot barrel out from receiver.
  3. Insert cartridge into barrel. Ensure cartridge is seated fully forward in rear of barrel.
  4. Pivot barrel into receiver until barrel locking lever engages barrel. (Note: There should be an audible click.)
  5. Move selector lever to "F" (fire position). Engage target.
  6. Move selector lever back to "S" (safe) position.

Unload and Clear an Unfired Round:

  1. Keep weapon pointed in safe direction, trigger finger outside trigger guard.
  2. Press upward on barrel release lever and pivot barrel out from receiver.
  3. Remove round or cartridge case by hand. Grasp rim. Pull rearward to remove.
  4. Pivot barrel into receiver until barrel release lever engages barrel.
  5. Ensure selector remains in "S" (safe) position.
✓ GO: Both parts correct, in sequence, within time
✗ NO-GO: Drops 40mm ammo, out of sequence, exceeds time

M249 Machine Gun — Clear, Disassemble, Assemble & Functions Check

▾
Task
Clear, completely disassemble, assemble, and perform a functions check on an M249
Standard (Disassemble)
Correctly clear and completely disassemble, matching groups with correct nomenclature, within 3 minutes
Standard (Assemble)
Correctly assemble and perform functions check within 3 minutes

Clear and Disassemble:

  1. Point weapon safe direction. Ensure safety is on FIRE. With right-hand palm up, pull and hold charging handle to rear. Place on SAFE.
  2. Return charging handle to locked position.
  3. Look away from feed tray cover. Squeeze latches, open feed tray cover assembly upward.
  4. Conduct five-point safety check: feed pawl assembly under cover; feed tray assembly; lift tray and inspect chamber; inspect magazine well; inspect space between bolt face and chamber; inspect under bolt and operating rod.
  5. Lower and close feed tray cover (securely locks).
  6. With right-hand palm up, pull and hold charging handle to rear. Place on FIRE. Press trigger with left hand, ease bolt forward with right hand to locked forward position. Release trigger.
  7. Disassemble into groups on correct nomenclature labels: (a) Driving spring and return rod/transfer mechanism assembly, separated; (b) Bolt, slide, piston assemblies, separated; (c) Heat shield, gas regulator, barrel; (d) Handguard and receiver assembly; (e) Buttstock and buffer assembly; (f) Trigger mechanism assembly; (g) Gas cylinder; (h) Bipod.

Assemble and Functions Check:

  1. Assemble the weapon (reverse of disassembly).
  2. Functions check — Point safe direction. Safety on FIRE. Pull and hold charging handle to rear while still holding.
  3. Squeeze trigger — weapon should NOT fire.
  4. Place on FIRE. Press trigger with left hand, ease bolt forward until locked forward position. Release trigger.
✓ GO: Correct nomenclature, in sequence, within 3 min each part

M18A1 Claymore Mine — Emplace, Fire & Recover

▾
Task
Employ a Claymore Mine. Recover a Claymore mine.
Standard (Emplace/Fire)
Correctly employ and fire, in sequence, within 10 minutes
Standard (Recover)
Correctly recover unfired mine, in sequence, within 5 minutes

Emplace and Fire:

  1. Inventory components: M57 firing device, M4 electric blasting cap assembly, M40 test set, M7 bandoleer.
  2. Select firing position with cover and unobstructed observation of emplacement site.
  3. Perform circuit test on M57 firing device using M40 test set. Move bail to FIRE. Depress handle — observe light. Candidate states "I see the light." Move bail to SAFE.
  4. Uncoil ~1 meter of wire from shorting plug end. At 1-meter mark, fold to create loop. Twist loop over index/middle fingers. Push through circle. Secure firing wire from blasting cap side to stake at firing position. Uncoil enough wire to place spool (with blasting cap inside) out of sight under sandbag/tree/hole.
  5. Remove M57 with M40 attached. Remove dust covers. Plug firing wire into test set. Move bail to FIRE. Depress — observe light. Candidate states "I see the light." Move bail to SAFE. Disconnect, replace covers, repack.
  6. Unroll firing wire from firing position to emplacement site. Assume prone. Open legs to 45 degrees facing enemy. Push legs ~1/3 into ground. Ensure face marked 'FRONT TOWARD ENEMY' faces enemy.
  7. Aim: select aiming point ~50 meters in front. Position eye ~6 inches from sight rear. Arm: remove blasting cap from spool, secure firing wire ~1 meter to rear. Unscrew shipping plug priming adapter. Slide slotted end onto firing wire of blasting cap. Pull excess wire through until blasting cap is firmly seated. Screw adapter into detonator well. Check aim unchanged.
  8. Camouflage munition and firing wire while moving back to firing position.
  9. Fire: seek cover, remove dust cover from firing device. Disconnect shorting plug/dust cover from end of firing wire, connect to firing device. Move bail to FIRE. State "CLAYMORE" twice and quickly squeeze handle.

Recover Unfired Mine:

  1. Ensure firing device bail is in SAFE. Disconnect from wire. Replace shorting plug/dust cover. Place in bandoleer. Untie firing wire from stake at firing site. Move to mine.
  2. Remove shipping plug priming adapter by unscrewing. Separate adapter and blasting cap. Reverse shipping plug, screw into detonator well.
  3. Remove firing wire from stake at mine site. Place blasting cap into wire connector end and roll onto wire container. Lift mine, secure folding legs. Repack mine and all accessories into M7 bandoleer.
✓ GO: Circuit tested, correctly emplaced/aimed/armed/fired within 10 min; recovered within 5 min
✗ NO-GO: Fails circuit test, incorrect orientation, exceeds time

M98 Javelin — Prepare to Fire & React to Misfire

▾
Task
Prepare M98 Javelin for firing. Perform immediate action for a misfire.
Standard (Prepare)
Prepare to fire within 3 minutes 30 seconds
Standard (Misfire IA)
Perform immediate action within 2 minutes

Prepare to Fire:

  1. Prepare CLU: ensure power switch OFF. Install CLU battery. Place round on ground, flat end caps down, latch assembly facing up. Kneel on left side of round at forward end, facing forward.
  2. Remove protective cover from CLU interface connector. Pull lanyard tab snug. Open day sight and NVS lens covers on CLU.
  3. Place CLU round interface bracket in round hooks. Engage CLU and round interface connectors by sliding forward and pressing down on CLU. Rock CLU side-to-side to confirm connected. Pick up Javelin.
  4. Remove forward end cap — pull locking pin straight up via wire rope, turn latch release counterclockwise, place cap on ground. Inspect seeker dome. Tip round down to allow debris to fall out. Position open end of round on forward end cap.
  5. Power CLU to NIGHT. Wait 2.5–3.5 minutes for NVS to cool. Select firing position.
  6. Verify CLU indicators are lit. Candidate verbalizes. Grader: "CLU indicators are flashing." Perform battery warm-up: turn to DAY for 30–60 sec. Grader: "30 seconds has elapsed." Turn OFF then back to NIGHT.
  7. Adjust diopter ring for best CLU display clarity. Candidate verbalizes "The Javelin is prepared to engage."
  8. Squeeze seeker trigger, wait 4 seconds, count aloud. Candidate states "Back blast area clear." Acquire target, lock on, squeeze fire trigger. Grader states "Misfire."

React to Misfire (Immediate Action):

  1. Release seeker and fire triggers, attempt to engage again per above. Grader states "Misfire."
  2. Keep Javelin pointed at target. Back blast area clear.
  3. Attempt to engage again. Grader states "Misfire."
  4. Turn CLU OFF. Note: leave in DAY position for ≥1 second before turning off.
  5. Set Javelin on ground pointed at enemy target, CLU handgrips facing up. Keep back blast area clear.
  6. Disconnect CLU from round. Move 25 meters away OR move round 25 meters from firing position. Candidate verbalizes.
  7. Obtain a replacement round. Candidate verbalizes.

Camouflage and Visual Signaling Techniques

▾
Task
Subtly employ realistic camouflage. Demonstrate 15 visual signaling techniques.
Standard (Camouflage)
Correctly apply within 10 minutes
Standard (Visual Signals)
Correctly demonstrate each of the 15 visual signaling techniques within 10 seconds of receiving prompt

Camouflage:

  1. Employ realistic camouflage that resembles the background without overdoing.
  2. Use natural or artificial materials to break up regular shapes, outlines, and equipment.
  3. Reduce possible shine by covering/removing items that reflect light.
  4. Blend colors with surroundings — objects should not contrast with background.
  5. Camouflage exposed skin: cover skin oils using paint sticks even with very dark skin. Use color chart when applying paint on face. Paint high, shiny areas (forehead, cheekbones, nose, ears, chin) with dark color. Paint low, shadow areas (around eyes, under nose, under chin) with light color. Paint exposed skin on neck, arms, and hands with irregular pattern. Note: Do NOT use oils, insect repellant, mud, motor oil, or field expedient paints for EIB.
  6. Camouflage uniform and helmet: roll sleeves down, fasten all buttons/zippers/Velcro. Attach leaves, grass, small branches to uniform/helmet to distort shapes and blend. Wear unstarched uniforms. Do not wear excessively faded/worn uniforms.
  7. Camouflage personal equipment: cover/remove shiny items, secure items that rattle, break up shape of large/bulky equipment.

Key Visual Signaling Techniques (15 will be tested from this list):

  1. Disperse: Extend arm horizontally. Wave arm and hand to front, left, right, and rear. Point toward direction of each movement.
  2. Assemble/Rally: Raise arm vertically overhead, turn palm to front, wave in large horizontal circles.
  3. Join me / Follow me / Come forward: Point toward person/unit, beckon by holding arm horizontally to front with palm up, motion toward body.
  4. Increase speed / Double time / Rush: Raise fist to shoulder, thrust fist upward to full extent of arm and back to shoulder level rapidly, several times.
  5. Quick time: Extend arm horizontally sideward, turn palm to front, wave arm slightly downward.
  6. Halt: Raise hand to shoulder height with palm facing forward, fingers together extended upward, hold.
  7. Freeze / Stop: Raise fist at shoulder level.
  8. Down / Take cover: Extend arm toward soldiers, palm down, move arm downward several times.
  9. Wedge formation: Extend both arms down and to the sides at 45 degrees.
  10. Column / File formation: Point arm overhead to rear.
  11. Line formation: Extend arms horizontally to sides, palms forward.
  12. Echelon right: Extend right arm 45 degrees down to right, left arm 45 degrees up to left.
  13. Echelon left: Extend left arm 45 degrees down to left, right arm 45 degrees up to right.
  14. Enemy in sight: Raise arm and fist to shoulder, point in direction of enemy.
  15. I am ready / All clear: Raise hand, palm outward, thumb and forefinger forming a circle, other fingers extended.
✓ GO: Camouflage applied within 10 min, each of 15 signals demonstrated within 10 seconds of prompt
✗ NO-GO: Incorrect camouflage application, misses signal within 10 seconds, uses prohibited materials

Resection (Map and Compass Method):

  1. Orient map on flat surface using compass. Identify ≥2 well-defined points on ground. Mark these on map.
  2. Plot back azimuths: determine magnetic azimuth from your location to one defined point. Convert to grid azimuth. Convert to back grid azimuth. Place index point of protractor on well-defined point. Align protractor's 0–180 degree line to top of map's North-South grid line. Ensure 0-degree mark points north. Place tick mark on map beside number on protractor corresponding to computed back grid azimuth. Draw straight line from well-defined point through tick mark and beyond. Repeat for each well-defined point.
  3. Identify the point where lines intersect as your location. Determine the six-digit grid coordinates to this location.

  1. 5 Major: Hill, Saddle, Valley, Ridge, Depression.
  2. 3 Minor: Draw, Spur, Cliff.
  3. 2 Supplementary: Cut, Fill.
  4. 6 Colors: Blue (hydrography/water); Black (cultural/manmade features, labels); Green (vegetation); Brown (relief features, contours, cultivated land on red-light-readable maps); Red (cultural features, populated areas, main roads, boundaries on older maps); Red-Brown (cultural features, all relief features, non-surveyed spot elevations, contour lines on red-light-readable maps).

  1. ⚠ Not Retestable

The 12-mile Foot March and Final Event is NOT retestable. All candidates must successfully complete the 12-mile Foot March in three hours or less, immediately followed by the Final Event, which must be started within five minutes of completing the march.

Conditions

Route Requirements

Route Standards

  • Route must be exactly 12 miles in length, clearly marked, easily identifiable by candidates
  • Length verified by unit personnel using two military issued GPS devices
  • Must be validated by the EIB Test Manager
  • Route should be closed to vehicular traffic; if not, positive safety control measures required
  • Medics stationed along route, ambulances available
  • Sufficient water points established IAW Commander's DRAW
  • Turn-around points must be easily recognizable and allow candidates to turn around without impeding each other
  • At least two official timing devices calibrated for the foot march

Ruck Training Tips

  • Build up progressively — follow the PT plan ruck schedule (3 mi → 5 mi → 6 mi → 8 mi)
  • Ensure ruck fits properly — tight enough not to bounce, padded hip belt carrying the weight
  • Break in your boots well before test day
  • Practice with the same weight you'll carry on test day
  • Know your pace — you need ~4.0 mph average to finish in 3 hours
  • Sock system — wool liner + moisture-wicking outer. Prevent blisters
  • Toenails trimmed — long toenails cause significant pain on long rucks
